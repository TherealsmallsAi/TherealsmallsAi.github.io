“The Orbital File.”

The FCC authorized SpaceX to put up to one million Starmind satellites in orbit — orbital AI data centers, the first authorization of its kind in the United States. Each carries about 250 kilowatts of compute, built with Nvidia. First launch in 2027 at the earliest, full build-out by 2040.

About the newsroom

One desk. One standard.

Small Talk AI News is run solo by Mike Price out of Canton, Ohio. It covers AI, AGI and ASI — the advancements, and the good things AI is doing — with a verification-first rule: every number is traced to its source before it goes out.

  • Verified before news. A claim ships only when its source checks out.
  • Company numbers are labeled. When a figure comes from the company itself — a benchmark, a price, a capability — the story says so, on the record.
  • No hype. No spec-sheet launches, no corny angles, no filler. One sharp, verified piece beats a day of noise.
  • Corrections are public. When a story is wrong, it gets fixed and the fix is part of the record.
